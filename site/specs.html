PocketOS

Tech Specs

A vivid 1.75-inch round AMOLED touchscreen, a precision aluminium case, microphones and a speaker, all in something that fits in your palm. Here’s everything inside, and what it means for you.

Made to be held.

  • 466 × 466 AMOLED display with capacitive touch
  • Rechargeable battery with USB-C charging
  • Dual microphones and a speaker for voice
  • 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. No app, account or subscription

In the box

    What you’ll need

    Home AssistantPocketOS controls your home through Home Assistant, running on your home network. It doesn’t work with Alexa, Google Home or Apple Home on their own.
    2.4 GHz Wi-FiProvided by almost every home router, usually alongside 5 GHz.
    Your phone, for setupIts camera and web browser join PocketOS to your Wi-Fi and connect Home Assistant. No app, and nothing to download.

    Everything else is in the box, including the USB-C charging cable. Plug it into any USB charger or computer to charge.

    Software and privacy

    • PocketOS comes installed and updates itself over Wi-Fi. Every update is signed.
    • Security updates until at least 31 December 2029. Security
    • No PocketOS account, subscription, advertising or tracking. Privacy
