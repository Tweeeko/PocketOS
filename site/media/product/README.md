# Product images

Concept renders of the PocketOS Device and its packaging, used on the Where to Buy page (`buy.html`).

To replace a render with a photograph, add the photo here and update its entry in
`site/assets/product-media.js`: change `src` and set `kind: 'photo'`, which removes the
"Concept render" label. Landscape 4:3 photos at 1600 × 1200 or larger look best. The page fits
any image into its frame without stretching, so other sizes won't break the layout.

The renders are made by `tools/renders/` (see its README).
