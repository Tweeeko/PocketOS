PocketOS Device

Meet PocketOS.Your home, at a glance.

A pocket-sized smart-home companion designed to bring your home together in one beautifully simple device.

Arrives ready to use, with PocketOS installed. Requires Home Assistant on your home network. What you’ll need

Made to be held.

A 1.75-inch round AMOLED touchscreen in an aluminium case, with two physical side buttons.

  • 466 × 466 AMOLED display with capacitive touch
  • Rechargeable battery with USB-C charging
  • Dual microphones and a speaker for voice
  • 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. No app, account or subscription
