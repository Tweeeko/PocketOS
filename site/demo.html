Try PocketOS

Use PocketOS and its settings page side by side, with a sample home. It all runs in your browser, so nothing you do here goes anywhere.

The settings page, as it appears on your phone

Things to try

  1. Open the menuSwipe up on the screen, then tap Lights.
  2. Dim a lightOpen Floor lamp and drag around the ring.
  3. Talk to your homeHold the Boot button, then let go.
  4. Find the settings codePress the Boot button once.
  5. Turn the screen offPress the Power button. Hold it for 6 seconds to switch off.
  6. Change the companionOn the settings page, open Look and choose another eye colour.
  7. Favourite or hide a devicePress and hold a device in a list, or tap ··· on its screen.
  8. Rename a deviceOn the settings page, open Devices and tap ✎. Reorder favourites there too.
  9. Leave the door unlockedOpen Locks, then Front door, and watch the companion.

The sample home is pretend. On a real PocketOS, devices and settings come from your own Home Assistant, and voice uses your own assistant.
