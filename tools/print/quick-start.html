Quick start

Your home, at a glance. Setting up takes about five minutes.

Home Assistant2.4 GHz Wi-FiYour phone
  1. Charge and switch onPlug PocketOS in with the USB-C cable. To switch it on, hold the Power button for 1 second.
  2. Join PocketOS from your phonePoint your phone’s camera at the code on PocketOS’s screen and tap to join. A setup page opens.
  3. Choose your home Wi-FiPick your network and enter its password. PocketOS joins it, and your phone goes back to your Wi-Fi.
  4. Connect Home AssistantOn the settings page, open Smart home and follow the steps. Your devices appear on PocketOS.

Safety and care

  • PocketOS has a built-in rechargeable lithium battery. Don’t open, crush, puncture or heat it.
  • Charge with the supplied cable from a USB charger or computer. Don’t leave it charging on soft furnishings.
  • Keep it dry and away from heat. Clean with a soft, dry cloth.
  • If it swells, gets hot, smells or is damaged, stop using it and contact us.

Don’t put PocketOS in household rubbish. Take it to an electrical recycling point.
